## overview
TheMarketProject remote

Commands act on the live bot. For Demo Trading add "demo" to a short command (/status demo) or "--env demo" to /mb (/mb --env demo status).

Read (safe):
/status - equity, window, risk, positions
/summary - daily summary of both bots
/forward - out-of-time check per strategy
/report - trades and PnL, last 24 h
/events - latest journal events
/ps - what runs on the computer
/market - market brief from public data
/check - API key and account checks
/strategies - configured and available strategies

Control (no orders):
/pause, /resume - stop or allow new entries
/mb risk-scale 0.3 - risk per trade multiplier
/mb auto on|off - let strategies trade or not
/mb strategies set a,b - choose strategies
/mb on-stop flatten|keep - positions at session end
/mb restart - reload the bot, keep positions

Trades and money (a second tap for close, flatten, transfer, session stop):
/mb propose SYMBOL long|short --stop P
/mb close SYMBOL - close one position
/mb flatten - close everything
/mb transfer 50 to-futures|to-spot
/mb session start|stop

/help <command> explains one command, e.g. /help propose.
Claude's proposals arrive with Run and Decline buttons and expire.
Free text is not read here: talk to Claude in the Claude app.

## status
/status, /mb status
The bot's state: equity and its wallets, the 24-hour window (start equity E0, target, loss line, mode NORMAL/CAUTION/PROTECT/DEFENSE/HALTED), open risk, positions with their stops, and recent events.

## summary
/summary, /mb summary [--hours 24]
Both bots in one message: equity against the window start, positions, trades and the exchange's PnL for the period, errors. Sent by itself every morning at SUMMARY_TIME.

## forward
/forward, /mb forward [--since 2026-10-01]
Research 13 in short: for each event strategy, the trades it would have made on events since the date, with the bot's own settings, against the research estimate. n: closed trades; open: still within their holding time. Sent by itself on Mondays. Takes up to a minute.

## report
/report, /mb report [--hours 24]
Trades, closes, and the exchange's own accounting for the period: realized PnL, fees, and funding per symbol.

## events
/events, /mb events [--limit 30]
The latest journal events: entries, stop moves, closes, commands, errors.

## ps
/ps, /mb ps
The mb processes on the computer: the Telegram remote, sessions, and bots, with their process ids and start times.

## market
/market, /mb market
A read-only brief from public data: trend, sentiment, funding extremes, and recent Binance announcements.

## check
/check, /mb check [--public-only]
Read-only checks: connectivity, clock, API key permissions (withdrawals must be off), wallets, and futures settings. Must show no FAIL before live trading.

## pause
/pause, /mb pause
No new positions. Open positions keep their stops and are still managed. Undo with /resume.

## resume
/resume, /mb resume
New positions are allowed again.

## risk-scale
/mb risk-scale <0..1>
Multiplies the risk per trade (3% of the window's start equity). Never above 1. Below about 0.3, a 20% stop gives positions under Binance's 5 USDT minimum, so entries are rejected; 40% stops need about 0.6. Kept across restarts.

## auto
/mb auto on|off
on: the configured strategies may open positions. off: the bot only manages risk on open positions. Kept across restarts.

## strategies
/strategies, /mb strategies show
/mb strategies set <name,name,...>
show: the configured strategies and every preset. set: choose them (comma-separated, no spaces); they take effect at the next start or /mb restart. Example: /mb strategies set delisting-short-4h,monitoring-short-1d

## on-stop
/mb on-stop flatten|keep
What happens to positions when the session ends: flatten closes them (default); keep leaves them open under their exchange stops. Applies at the next start or restart.

## restart
/mb restart
Restarts the bot on the current code. Positions stay open under their exchange stops and are taken over by the new bot. Settings (risk scale, auto, strategies) are kept.

## propose
/mb propose SYMBOL long|short --stop PRICE [--target PRICE] [--why TEXT]
A manual trade. The risk manager sizes it (risk per trade over the stop distance) or rejects it with the reasons; it gets an exchange-side stop, and a take-profit if --target is given.
Example: /mb propose SOLUSDT short --stop 182.5 --why "test"

## close
/mb close SYMBOL
Closes one position at market and cancels its orders. Asks for a second tap.

## flatten
/mb flatten
Closes every position and cancels every order, even without a running bot. Asks for a second tap.

## transfer
/mb transfer AMOUNT to-futures|to-spot [--asset USDT]
Moves funds between the spot and USD-M futures wallets of the live account. Asks for a second tap. Withdrawals are impossible: the API key has no withdrawal permission.

## session
/mb session start
/mb session stop
start: starts the bot in the background; it lasts until stop, independent of the remote and of Claude. stop: closes every position and order, then stops the bot; asks for a second tap.
